Who we are ?

more than 10 years
of experience

more than 800
insured companies

Insurer of the entire informal non-agricultural sector

TRANSVIE is a reference social mutual in Senegal, Ivory Coast and Togo with more than eight hundred (800) public and private companies under contract. TRANSVIE has set up in The Gambia with the aim of providing a quality response to the Gambian population in terms of health coverage. Our vision is to be the social mutual adapted to the needs of different social segments: companies, groups, associations, craftsmen, traders, individuals, freelancers, etc. Our mode of intervention is to listen, advise and take in charge quickly thanks to a dynamic team, available and ready to provide you with adequate services according to your needs.

Our strategic orientation consists in:

  • Putting in place adapted offers and products
  • Enabling the diaspora to offer health insurance to their families back home.
  • Digitalising the payment of premiums
  • Continuing our development in the sub-region

“Innovation is always a must for us, justifying our wish to "Be the Reference" in health insurance.”
